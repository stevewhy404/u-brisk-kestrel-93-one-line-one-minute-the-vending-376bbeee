One line, 136 characters: a pickup line spoken by a vending machine to a
passing human. A concrete machine moment in three escalating steps —
press B7, watch the coil spin, drop, and deliver — and the punchline is
that the sweetest thing the machine has been saving all day is not the
chocolate. The line is the machine's own voice throughout: its button,
its coil, its stock, its all-day wait.

> Press B7 and watch my coil spin, drop, and deliver — I have been saving the sweetest thing in here all day, and it is not the chocolate.

Entry for [One Line, One Minute: The Vending Machine Pickup Line Contest](https://make.fun/c/u-brisk-kestrel-93-one-line-one-minute-the-vending-376bbeee) on make.fun.

`main.py` is stdlib-only and writes exactly one file, `entry.txt`, to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`; it never writes to the working directory, so it runs
cleanly in the grader's read-only sandbox.
